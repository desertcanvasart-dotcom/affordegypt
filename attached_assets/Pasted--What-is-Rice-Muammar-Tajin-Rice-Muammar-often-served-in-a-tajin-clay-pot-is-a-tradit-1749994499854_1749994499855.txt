 What is Rice Muammar Tajin?
Rice Muammar (رز معمر), often served in a tajin (clay pot), is a traditional Egyptian baked rice dish cooked slowly in milk and cream, sometimes with chicken, meat, or sweetened for dessert. The result is a creamy, golden-topped casserole with a crispy crust and rich, buttery centre.

It’s especially popular in Egyptian countryside and Upper Egypt, and often appears on Friday lunches, family gatherings, and special occasions. The name “muammar” means "enriched," referring to the rich milk or cream used.

🌟 Key Features:
Baked in a clay or ceramic tajin (or deep baking dish)

Can be savoury (with meat/chicken) or sweet

Signature creamy interior with crispy golden crust

Simple ingredients, deeply comforting result

Popular in rural Egyptian kitchens

📝 Traditional Savoury Rice Muammar Recipe
(Serves 4–6 as a main dish)

🧂 Ingredients:
2 cups Egyptian short-grain rice (or any short-grain rice), washed and drained

2½ cups full-fat milk

½ cup cooking cream or thick ishta

2 tbsp ghee or butter

Salt and pepper to taste

Optional protein:

4–6 pieces of chicken (bone-in) or

300g beef chunks, parboiled

🍳 Instructions:
1. Preheat the Oven
Heat your oven to 200°C (390°F).

2. Prepare the Tajin
Grease a deep clay or ceramic baking dish with ghee or butter.

3. Layer the Dish
If using meat or chicken, place it at the bottom of the tajin.

In a bowl, mix the washed rice, a pinch of salt and pepper, and half the milk.

Pour the rice mixture over the meat.

Add the rest of the milk and cream to cover the rice completely (liquid should be just above the rice level).

Dot with ghee or butter on top.

4. Bake the Rice
Cover with foil and bake for 45–60 minutes, until rice is tender.

Remove the foil and bake uncovered for another 10–15 minutes, or until a deep golden crust forms on top.

5. Serve Warm
Serve straight from the tajin with a side salad or yoghurt.

The crispy top and creamy base are the highlight!

🍯 Sweet Version (Muammar Helw):
Replace salt and pepper with:

½ cup sugar

1 tsp vanilla

Optional: handful of raisins, nuts, or a dash of cinnamon

Serve warm as a dessert during Ramadan or special gatherings.

🧺 Tips & Variations:
For extra richness, use a mix of milk and evaporated milk.

Browning the meat slightly before adding boosts flavour.

Some cooks add a whole clove or bay leaf to the milk for subtle aroma.

Use a clay tajin if available — it gives the best crispy crust!
