## 🥙 What is Hawawshi?

**Hawawshi (حواوشي)** is a classic **Egyptian meat-stuffed bread**, crisp on the outside and juicy inside, often compared to a Middle Eastern-style meat pie or spiced burger in a pita. It's essentially **spiced minced meat**, usually beef or lamb, mixed with vegetables and herbs, then **stuffed into baladi bread (Egyptian pita)** or dough and baked or grilled to perfection.

### 🔥 Signature Traits:

* **Crispy and golden** on the outside
* **Juicy, spiced meat** filling inside
* Packed with **onions, peppers, chillies**, and **warm spices**
* Popular as a **street snack**, family meal, or party dish

---

## 🌶️ Typical Ingredients:

* Ground beef or lamb
* Onion, bell pepper, garlic, green chilli
* Parsley or coriander
* Spices like cumin, paprika, cinnamon, black pepper, and allspice
* Baladi bread or pita (or homemade dough)

---

## 📝 Traditional Hawawshi Recipe (Egyptian Style)

### ✨ Ingredients (for 4–5 servings):

#### For the filling:

* 500g (1.1 lb) **minced beef or lamb**
* 1 large **onion**, finely chopped
* 1 medium **bell pepper**, finely chopped
* 1–2 **green chillies** (optional), finely chopped
* 2–3 cloves **garlic**, minced
* 1 small bunch **parsley**, finely chopped
* 1 tsp **ground cumin**
* 1 tsp **paprika**
* ½ tsp **ground cinnamon**
* ½ tsp **allspice**
* Salt & black pepper to taste

#### For the bread:

* 5 small rounds of **baladi bread** or **pita**
* 2–3 tbsp **butter or oil** (for brushing)

---

### 🍳 Instructions:

#### 1. **Prepare the Filling:**

In a large bowl, mix together all the filling ingredients until well combined. You don’t need to cook the meat first—it will cook inside the bread.

#### 2. **Stuff the Bread:**

* Carefully open the pita bread halfway to create a pocket (if using fresh baladi bread).
* Stuff each with 2–3 tablespoons of the meat mixture, spreading it evenly to cover the inner surface.

#### 3. **Brush & Wrap:**

* Lightly brush the outside of the bread with butter or oil for extra crispiness.
* Optional: wrap in foil if baking, or place directly on a hot skillet.

#### 4. **Cook:**

* **Oven method**: Bake at 200°C (400°F) for 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway until golden and crispy.
* **Skillet method**: Cook on a non-stick or cast-iron skillet over medium heat, pressing gently, for about 5–7 minutes per side.

#### 5. **Serve Hot:**

Slice in halves or quarters and serve with **tahini, pickles, or salad**.

---

## 🍴 Variations:

* **Alexandrian Hawawshi**: Usually spicier and cooked in freshly kneaded dough instead of pita.
* Add **cheese** for a modern twist.
* Use **whole wheat pita** for a lighter version.
