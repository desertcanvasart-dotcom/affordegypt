# 🥙 **Egyptian Street Food: A Culinary Adventure Guide**

Egypt’s street food scene is a **symphony of flavours**, combining ancient culinary traditions with quick, modern-day indulgence. Whether you’re navigating the bustling alleys of Cairo or enjoying a seaside stroll in Alexandria, street food is your most delicious—and budget-friendly—passport to understanding Egypt.

---

## 📍 **Where to Find Egyptian Street Food**

| City                     | Best Spots                                                         |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Cairo**                | Downtown (Tahrir Sq., El-Mounira), Giza, Sayeda Zeinab, El Hussein |
| **Alexandria**           | Mansheya, Bahary Corniche, Miami Area, El-Raml Station             |
| **Luxor**                | Luxor Souq, Salah El Din Street, Nile Corniche                     |
| **Aswan**                | Souq Area, Corniche El Nile, El Tahrir Street                      |
| **Siwa Oasis**           | Siwa Market, El Maraqi District                                    |
| **Port Said / Ismailia** | Near ports and old markets                                         |

🟡 **Tip:** Look for long queues of locals — it’s a sign the food is both good and safe.

---

## 💸 **Why Street Food is Perfect for Travellers**

* **Extremely affordable**: Most items cost between **5–30 EGP** (≈\$0.15–\$1 USD)
* **Fresh & Fast**: Cooked in front of you, often piping hot
* **Deeply local**: Recipes passed down for generations
* **Vegetarian-friendly**: Many meat-free options available
* **Cultural immersion**: Street food is how Egyptians connect, talk, and relax

---

## 🧭 **Top Street Foods to Try in Egypt**

### 1. 🇪🇬 **Koshari (كشري)**

* **What it is**: A carb-packed dish of rice, lentils, chickpeas, pasta, crispy onions, and tomato-vinegar sauce
* **Best in**: Cairo (Abou Tarek, Koshary El Tahrir)
* **Cost**: 15–25 EGP for a full bowl
* **Traveller Tip**: Ask for “shatta” (spicy sauce) if you enjoy heat

---

### 2. 🧆 **Ta’ameya (Egyptian Falafel)**

* **What it is**: Fried patties made of **fava beans** (not chickpeas), with herbs and sesame coating
* **Usually served with**: Baladi bread, salad, pickles
* **Cost**: 1–2 EGP per piece / Sandwich \~5–10 EGP
* **Best in**: Local breakfast carts across Cairo and Alexandria
* **Vegan-Friendly**: 100% plant-based and filling

---

### 3. 🫓 **Ful Medames (فول مدمس)**

* **What it is**: Slow-cooked fava beans with olive oil, cumin, lemon juice
* **Street Style**: Served in sandwiches with pickled vegetables or eggs
* **Best in**: Breakfast carts and working-class cafés
* **Cost**: 5–10 EGP per sandwich

---

### 4. 🍖 **Shawarma (شاورما)**

* **What it is**: Marinated beef or chicken sliced from a vertical rotisserie
* **Served with**: Garlic sauce (chicken), tahini (beef), wrapped in pita or saj
* **Cost**: 15–30 EGP per wrap
* **Best in**: Syrian-style shawarma kiosks in Cairo, Alexandria, and Hurghada

---

### 5. 🍟 **Hawawshi (حواوشي)**

* **What it is**: Spiced minced beef baked inside baladi bread
* **Texture**: Crispy on the outside, juicy inside
* **Cost**: 15–25 EGP per sandwich
* **Pro Tip**: Ask for “balady” version – more traditional and flavourful

---

### 6. 🍗 **Grilled Liver & Sausage (كبدة وسجق)**

* **Served as**: Sandwiches or platters with fries, salad, and tahini
* **Popular in**: Alexandria and Port Said
* **Cost**: 20–30 EGP
* **Caution**: Avoid if you have a sensitive stomach or during extreme heat

---

### 7. 🧀 **Roumy Cheese Sandwiches**

* **What it is**: Local hard cheese grilled in baladi bread
* **Great for**: Breakfast on-the-go or midnight snack
* **Cost**: 7–15 EGP

---

### 8. 🍩 **Desserts & Sweets**

* **Basbousa** – Semolina cake with sugar syrup
* **Zalabya (Luqmat al-Qadi)** – Mini fried dough balls drizzled in honey or sugar
* **Sweet Potato (بطاطا)** – Roasted and sold from push carts, often topped with honey or nuts
* **Cost Range**: 5–20 EGP

## 🧊 **Drinks to Pair with Street Food**

| Drink                      | What it is                             | Where to Find                 |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Sobia**                  | Coconut milk & rice drink              | Ramadan carts, markets        |
| **Sugarcane Juice (Asab)** | Fresh-pressed, hydrating               | Juice shops, street carts     |
| **Tamarind (Tamr Hindi)**  | Sweet-sour drink with dates & tamarind | Ramadan time, old cafés       |
| **Hibiscus (Karkadeh)**    | Deep red, tangy flower-based drink     | Particularly popular in Aswan |
| **Tea with Mint**          | Egypt’s go-to warm street drink        | Everywhere—just ask!          |

💧**Note**: Avoid tap water and ice; opt for sealed bottled water (≈5 EGP)

---

## 🧼 **Street Food Safety Tips for Travellers**

| Safety Tip                        | Reason                                |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Eat where locals queue            | Indicates high turnover and freshness |
| Avoid dairy-heavy foods in summer | Heat may spoil cheese/yogurt quickly  |
| Choose piping hot food            | Kills most bacteria                   |
| Bring your own wipes/sanitizer    | Street vendors often lack handwashing |
| Stick to cooked vegetables        | Raw veg may be washed with tap water  |

---

## 🧳 **What to Pack for a Street Food Adventure**

* **Tissues/wet wipes**
* **Hand sanitizer**
* **Reusable water bottle**
* **Anti-diarrhea tablets (just in case)**
* **Small notes and coins** for tipping and exact change

## 💬 **Useful Arabic Phrases**

| English           | Arabic (Transliteration)      | Use Case               |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| How much is this? | بكام ده؟ (bekam dah?)         | Asking for price       |
| No spice, please  | من غير شطة (men gheer shatta) | Mild version of food   |
| Thank you         | شكراً (shokran)               | General politeness     |
| Delicious!        | لذيذ جداً! (lazeez gidan!)    | Compliment to the cook |
