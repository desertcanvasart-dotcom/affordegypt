What is Ful Medames?
Ful Medames (فول مدمس) is Egypt’s most iconic breakfast dish, made from slow-cooked fava beans and typically seasoned with olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, and spices. It’s deeply embedded in Egyptian daily life — from simple street carts in Cairo to home kitchens across the country.

Despite its simplicity, Ful is versatile and rich in protein and fibre, making it both nutritious and filling. It's usually eaten with baladi bread, boiled eggs, pickles, and sometimes taameya (Egyptian falafel).

🌟 Key Features:
Texture: Creamy or chunky (depending on preference)

Flavour: Earthy, garlicky, lemony, and sometimes spicy

Vegan & healthy: Packed with fibre and protein

National favourite: Enjoyed daily by millions — especially at breakfast

📝 Traditional Egyptian Ful Medames Recipe (Serves 4)
🧂 Ingredients:
2 cups cooked fava beans (or 1 large can, drained)

2–3 cloves garlic, minced

2–3 tbsp olive oil (or vegetable oil)

Juice of 1 lemon (or more to taste)

½ tsp ground cumin

Salt and black pepper to taste

Optional: pinch of chili flakes, paprika, or crushed tomato

🥄 Optional Garnishes & Sides:
Diced tomatoes, onions, or parsley

Hard-boiled eggs

Pickled vegetables (torshi)

Baladi bread or warm pita

A drizzle of tahini sauce or chopped green chilies

🍳 Instructions:
1. Warm the Beans
In a small saucepan, add the cooked fava beans and a splash of water. Heat gently over medium heat until warmed through.

2. Mash (if desired)
Use a fork or potato masher to lightly mash the beans. You can keep them chunky or mash them into a smoother paste depending on your preference.

3. Add Seasoning
Stir in:

Minced garlic

Cumin

Lemon juice

Salt & pepper

Olive oil

Simmer for 2–3 more minutes to blend flavours.

4. Serve Warm
Transfer to a bowl or plate. Garnish with chopped tomatoes, onions, parsley, or tahini drizzle if desired. Serve with warm baladi bread.

🍴 Popular Egyptian Ful Variations:
Ful Eskandrani (Alexandrian-style): Served cold, often with chili, tomatoes, parsley, and vinegar.

Ful with boiled eggs: Classic street-style breakfast.

Ful bel Dabba (with butter): A creamy, indulgent version often served in restaurants.

Spicy Ful: Add harissa or fresh chili oil for heat lovers.
