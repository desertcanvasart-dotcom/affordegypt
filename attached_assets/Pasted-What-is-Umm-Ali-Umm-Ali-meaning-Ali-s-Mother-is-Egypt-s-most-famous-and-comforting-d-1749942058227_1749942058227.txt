What is Umm Ali?
Umm Ali (أم علي) — meaning “Ali’s Mother” — is Egypt’s most famous and comforting dessert. It's a rich, creamy bread pudding-style dish, made by layering pastry or bread with nuts, milk, cream, and sugar, then baking until bubbling and golden on top.

It’s warm, indulgent, and associated with celebrations, especially during Ramadan, feasts, weddings, and special occasions. With its crispy top and soft custard base, Umm Ali is often called the Egyptian version of bread pudding, but with a Middle Eastern flair — loaded with nuts, coconut, and sometimes raisins.

🌟 Key Features:
Served warm, straight from the oven

Crispy, golden top; creamy and soft underneath

Made with puff pastry, phyllo, or bread

Includes milk, cream, sugar, and nuts

No eggs – unlike Western bread puddings

Rich, festive, and deeply satisfying

📝 Traditional Umm Ali Recipe (Serves 6–8)
🧂 Ingredients:
1 sheet puff pastry (or use phyllo, stale croissants, or sweet bread)

3 cups full-fat milk

1 cup heavy cream (or whipped cream for topping)

½ cup sugar

½ cup mixed nuts (pistachios, almonds, walnuts), chopped

¼ cup shredded coconut (unsweetened)

¼ cup raisins or sultanas (optional)

1 tsp vanilla extract (or ¼ tsp cardamom powder)

A pinch of cinnamon (optional)

🍳 Instructions:
1. Prepare the Pastry Base
Bake puff pastry according to package directions, then break it into large bite-sized flakes.

Alternative: Use stale croissants, toast, or phyllo – tear into pieces and lightly toast in oven until golden.

2. Assemble the Dish
In a baking dish, scatter the broken pastry.

Sprinkle evenly with nuts, raisins, and coconut.

3. Prepare the Milk Mixture
In a saucepan, heat the milk, sugar, and vanilla (or cardamom) until just below boiling.

Pour the hot milk gently over the pastry-nut mixture in the baking dish.

Let the pastry soak for 5–10 minutes.

4. Add the Cream Topping
Spoon or pour the heavy cream on top (do not stir).

Optional: Sprinkle with a bit of cinnamon for extra aroma.

5. Bake the Umm Ali
Bake at 200°C (390°F) for about 20–25 minutes, until the top is golden and bubbly.

For a crispier top, broil for an extra 2–3 minutes at the end (watch closely!).

6. Serve Warm
Best enjoyed fresh out of the oven.

Optional: Garnish with crushed pistachios or a drizzle of condensed milk for extra indulgence.

🔁 Variations:
Use cream-soaked toast for a more rustic version.

Add rosewater or orange blossom water for an Arabian twist.

For a richer version, add a few spoons of sweetened condensed milk to the milk mixture.
