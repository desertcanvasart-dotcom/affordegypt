4 | Visual & Interaction Details
Sticky CTA bar (mobile only)
After 180 px scroll, show a slim bar with the two primary CTAs; boosts conversion on long pages.

Teal > Beige gradient buttons
• Solid teal (#19A974) for primary, 8 px radius, shadow 2 dp
• Outline beige for secondary – keeps visual hierarchy clear.

Step cards
Convert the translucent rectangles into glass-blur cards (backdrop-filter) to modernise. Add tiny numbers in circles (① ② ③) and animate them upward on page load.

Icon set consistency
Use a single library (e.g., Lucide) so line weight matches across car, guide, ticket, etc.
