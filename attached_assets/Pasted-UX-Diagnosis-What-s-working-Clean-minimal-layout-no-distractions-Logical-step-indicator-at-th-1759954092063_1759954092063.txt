UX Diagnosis

What’s working

Clean, minimal layout — no distractions.

Logical step indicator at the top (great for guiding progress).

Strong CTA (“Continue to Destinations”) at the bottom.

Simple data entry (only two fields — low friction).

What’s not working

The layout feels empty and mechanical, lacking emotional or visual depth.

Hierarchy between headings, form, and CTAs is weak — everything merges visually.

The “Private Service” and “Secure Pricing” boxes don’t stand out as trust builders; they feel like filler text.

No visual cue connects this step to the travel experience (you lose that emotional trigger that makes people want to continue).

The stepper (progress bar) feels disconnected — no animation or visual link between stages.

✏️ UI & Layout Redesign Recommendations
1. Introduce Visual Warmth & Hierarchy

Current issue: Text-heavy, white-on-white layout with minimal contrast.

Fix:

Add a soft background tint to the main container (e.g., #F8FAFB or very light beige to match Egyptian tones).

Keep the card white with subtle drop shadow (0 2px 10px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)), rounded corners 12px.

Increase top/bottom padding for better breathing room (32–40px).

Make the section title more inviting:

✨ Let’s Plan Your Egypt Adventure
Tell us a few basics to begin building your journey.

2. Rework Step Indicator (Progress Bar)

Current issue: The progress steps are tiny and visually distant from the form.

Fix:

Add a colored progress bar under the step icons to show advancement (like Airbnb or Booking.com).

Use animation to highlight the active step when loading.

Simplify text labels: “Overview → Destinations → Add-ons → Review → Checkout”.

Consider using Egypt-themed icons (pyramid, map pin, add-on, receipt, checkmark).

3. Enhance Form Card Visuals

Fix the flat inputs:

Give input fields slightly thicker borders and more depth (border: 1.5px solid #E0E0E0).

On focus, use accent color (#008C86) with a glowing shadow (0 0 0 2px rgba(0,140,134,0.15)).

Use icons in fields (calendar, person) with subtle animations when focused.

Spacing between fields: at least 24px.

Optional addition: A small line under the headline:

“Takes less than 30 seconds to start your quote.”

4. Upgrade the Trust Elements

Right now, “Private Service” and “100% Secure & Transparent Pricing” feel like filler boxes.

New layout:
Convert them into horizontal badges with icons and microtext:

[⭐ Private Service]  All tours include your own vehicle & guide
[🔒 Transparent Pricing]  Real prices in EGP, no hidden fees


Use colored icon backgrounds (soft teal for private service, soft gray for security).

Make them horizontally aligned and responsive.

Add micro-motion: on hover, icon enlarges slightly.

5. Emotional Visual (Optional but Powerful)

To add emotional pull:

Add a small image banner or hero thumbnail above the title — maybe a faded shot of a traveler overlooking the Nile or pyramids.

Alternatively, place a subtle watermark (outline of pyramids or palm trees) in the background of the card for brand warmth.

This keeps it light but adds personality.

6. CTA & Interaction Design

Current CTA: “Continue to Destinations” — flat color and generic.

Upgrade:

Gradient or elevated button (background: linear-gradient(90deg, #008C86, #00A9A1)).

Add arrow icon → (e.g., “Continue to Destinations →”).

Hover state: soft lift animation + gradient shift.

Disable state: 40% opacity until both fields filled.

Optional secondary CTA: “Need Help? Chat with Our Team” (small text link below button with WhatsApp icon).

📱 Mobile Experience

Stack content vertically with centered alignment.

Make progress bar sticky at top with animation as user fills.

Replace cards with toggleable accordions (Private Service → expand for explanation).

Floating CTA at bottom: “Next Step →” always visible once fields are filled.

✨ Micro-interactions to Humanise It

Animated icons: calendar flips, traveler icon fills when selected.

Subtle fade-in for the form after the step loads (adds life).

Inline feedback: if date field empty → gentle reminder (“Please choose a start date to continue”).
