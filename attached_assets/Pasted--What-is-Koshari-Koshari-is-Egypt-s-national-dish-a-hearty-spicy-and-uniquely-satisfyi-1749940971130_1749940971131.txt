🍲 What is Koshari?
Koshari (كشري) is Egypt’s national dish — a hearty, spicy, and uniquely satisfying street food made by layering lentils, rice, pasta, chickpeas, and crispy fried onions, then drenching it in garlicky tomato sauce and an optional spicy vinegar-chili dressing.

What makes Koshari truly special is not just its flavour but its contrast of textures — soft grains, firm pasta, crunchy onions — all unified by tangy, savoury sauces.

Though humble in origin, Koshari is a symbol of Egyptian ingenuity, often served in homes, street stalls, and restaurants from Cairo to Aswan.

🌟 Why It’s Loved
Hearty & filling — a full meal in one bowl

Naturally vegan (no meat or dairy)

Layered textures: crispy, soft, chewy

Bold, spicy, tangy flavours

Affordable & widely available — the ultimate Egyptian street comfort food

📝 Traditional Egyptian Koshari Recipe (Serves 4–6)
🧂 Ingredients
Base:
1 cup brown lentils, rinsed

1 cup white rice

1 cup small pasta (like elbow or ditalini)

1 cup chickpeas (cooked or canned)

2 medium onions, very thinly sliced

Oil (for frying the onions)

Tomato Sauce:
2 tbsp vegetable oil

4 cloves garlic, minced

1 small onion, grated (optional)

400g (14 oz) canned crushed tomatoes or tomato puree

2 tbsp white vinegar

1 tsp ground cumin

1/2 tsp chili flakes (or to taste)

Salt & black pepper to taste

Spicy Vinegar Sauce (Da’a):
3 tbsp white vinegar

3 cloves garlic, minced

1/2 tsp cumin

1/4 tsp cayenne pepper or chili powder

1/2 cup water

Salt to taste

🍳 Instructions
1. Cook the Lentils:
Boil lentils in salted water until just tender (about 20 minutes). Drain and set aside.

2. Make the Rice:
Cook the rice in 2 cups of water with a little salt until fluffy. You can cook it together with the lentils in one pot for extra flavour.

3. Cook the Pasta:
Boil the pasta separately, drain, and set aside.

4. Prepare the Chickpeas:
If using canned, rinse and warm them in water. Set aside.

5. Fry the Onions:
In a deep pan, fry the thinly sliced onions in oil until deep golden and crispy (around 10–15 minutes). Drain on paper towels.

6. Make the Tomato Sauce:
In a saucepan, heat 2 tbsp oil and sauté garlic (and onion if using).

Add crushed tomatoes, vinegar, cumin, chili flakes, salt, and pepper.

Simmer for 15–20 minutes until thickened. Adjust seasoning.

7. Make the Spicy Vinegar Sauce (Da’a):
Lightly sauté the garlic in a bit of oil.

Add vinegar, water, cumin, cayenne, and salt.

Let simmer for 3–5 minutes.

🥄 To Serve:
Layer in a deep bowl:

Rice

Lentils

Pasta

Chickpeas

Tomato sauce

Crispy onions

Drizzle with Da’a (optional spicy vinegar sauce)

🇪🇬 Koshari Tips & Variations:
Some versions include a dash of baharat (Egyptian spice mix).

Make extra fried onions – they always disappear first!

You can prepare all elements in advance and assemble just before serving.

Reheats well, making it a great meal prep dish.
